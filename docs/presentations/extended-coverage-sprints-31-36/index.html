OPEN FNR / Sprints 31-36

Расширенное покрытие прогнозирования и пополнения

Итоговый блок закрывает procurement optimization, shelf space, capacity/workload, supply chain diagnostics, supplier collaboration, true inventory и store management. Все процессы заведены через OPEN FNR Process Engine: BPMN, DMN и CMMN.

Что добавлено

Procurement Optimization

Выбор поставщика, target share, supplier order export и supplier constraint case.

Shelf Space

Планограмма, display capacity, direct-to-shelf и shelf capacity exception.

Capacity And Workload

Перегрузка РЦ/транспорта/приемки, smoothing preview и TMS export.

Supply Chain Diagnostics

Root cause card, evidence trail, linked objects и exception from insight.

Supplier Collaboration

Forecast sharing, supplier confirmation, performance и shortage risk.

True Inventory

Virtual stock, confidence, store tasks, promo display confirmation и store feedback.
