OPEN FNR Sprint 29: Observability And Support

Аннотация: тестируем эксплуатационный контур: alert, incident, runbook, escalation, searchable logs and SLA severity. Сценарий: failed ERP export создает SEV2 alert и incident.

UI-тестирование

  1. Открываем Ops Dashboard.
  2. Проверяем alert list: publication-export, SEV2, runbook publication-export-failure.md.
  3. Проверяем incident card: owner L2, SLA 60 min, timeline.
  4. Проверяем actions: Acknowledge, Escalate, Resolve.
  5. Проверяем logs panel: trace-export-001 связан с failed export.
